## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a visceral, high-stakes aesthetic for an interactive graphic novel survival experience. It merges dark atmospheric graphic illustration with military-grade tactical field telemetry. 

The interface reflects urgency, resource depletion, and gritty storytelling. Visual cues evoke hand-drawn inked panels combined with a translucent, tactical survival HUD. The aesthetic relies on moody, charcoal-tinted dark backdrops, razor-sharp borders, condensed technical typography, and luminous hazard accents that cut through the shadows. 

Every UI element should feel field-fabricated and urgent—neither sterile nor arcade-like. Surfaces evoke scratched ballistic glass, field monitors, and printed graphic novel gutters.

## Colors
The palette is built upon a layered hierarchy of desaturated tactical darks punctuated by luminous operational indicators:

- **Primary Tactical Amber (`#E5A93C`, highlight `#F4C458`):** Denotes focus, primary user agency, active selections, fuel, and time-remaining telemetry.
- **Secondary Telemetry Cyan (`#4A90E2`):** Reserved for technical logs, intelligence data, radio transmissions, and environmental diagnostics.
- **Tertiary Hazard Crimson (`#D64045`):** Expresses mortal threat, critical structural failure, trauma, bleed-out timers, and irreversible choices.
- **Vitality Green (`#48BB78`):** Used strictly for stabilized states, successful rationing, medical intervention, and biological status.
- **Neutral Charcoal Layers (`#121316` Canvas, `#1A1C22` Base Panels, `#262930` Elevated Surfaces):** Provides a heavy, ink-rich graphic novel backdrop that preserves dark adaptation and heightens dread.

Text colors strictly contrast: Primary text uses `#F0F1F3` (ink off-white), secondary text `#9DA3AE`, and disabled or passive data `#4F5563`.

## Typography
Typographic rules reflect survival field manuals and graphic novel narration:

1. **Hierarchy & Tone:** Headlines and dramatic narration use **Barlow Condensed** in uppercase with expanded tracking for maximum urgency and dense information architecture. Dialogue and field journals utilize regular sentence casing.
2. **Telemetry & Metadata:** Timers, coordinates, ammo counts, inventory volumes, and system alerts rely strictly on **Space Mono**. All uppercase telemetry labels (`label-sm`, `label-md`) must feature generous tracking to mirror screen-printed mil-spec stencil markings.
3. **Pacing:** Narrative text blocks must maintain tight, controlled line heights to reinforce an enclosed, claustrophobic atmosphere.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout simulates a hybrid between an asymmetric graphic novel page and an integrated survival helmet HUD.

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views implement a 12-column fluid grid framed within a fixed aspect containment container with safe padding to support narrative framing. Mobile collapses to a single-column stacked format with a persistent tactical status bar at the top and critical decision triggers anchored to the bottom.
- **Graphic Gutters:** Content panels use fixed `gutter` divisions that double as graphic panel dividers, evoking comic book frames.
- **Rhythm & Density:** High data density is balanced by dark negative space around primary story illustrations. Telemetry modules cluster tightly with `space-xs` and `space-sm` gaps, while overarching narrative branches and interactive modules split using `space-lg` and `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through atmospheric lighting and layered paneling rather than traditional soft drop shadows:

- **HUD Glassmorphism:** Overlay surfaces use a base of `#1A1C22` rendered at 80%–90% opacity paired with a subtle background blur (`backdrop-blur: 8px`). This reveals shifting graphic novel background art while maintaining legibility.
- **Inked Outlines & Tactical Borders:** Modals, item slots, and HUD components use crisp 1px borders colored `#262930` or semi-opaque amber (`rgba(229, 169, 60, 0.3)`). Critical items feature an offset double-line border mimicking technical drafting marks.
- **Atmospheric Vignette:** A full-screen radial vignette gradient overlays the perimeter (`radial-gradient(circle, transparent 60%, rgba(10, 11, 13, 0.85) 100%)`) to create tunnel vision and center visual attention.
- **Tactical Backlighting:** Active modal overlays or life-threatening choices emit a subtle directional edge-glow using Hazard Crimson or Amber with tight blur radiuses (0px to 8px) and 25% opacity.

## Shapes
Shapes must feel machined, resilient, and angular:

- **Corner Treatment:** The system enforces minimal corner rounding (`roundedness: 1`, 0.25rem base radius). Corner radiuses never exceed 0.5rem, preserving an industrial, technical look.
- **Beveled & Chamfered Geometry:** Primary tactical buttons, dialogue decision nodes, and HUD gauges leverage 45-degree chamfered corner cuts (clip-path) on top-right and bottom-left edges to evoke rugged military hardware.
- **Framing Brackets:** Key cards and dialog frames utilize decorative geometric corner tick-marks (L-brackets) to reinforce the sense of an optical rangefinder or field instrument.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Choices
- **Primary Survival Action:** Chamfered button with an amber background (`#E5A93C`), deep charcoal text (`#121316`), bold condensed font, and high-contrast tactile feedback. Hover states flare into `#F4C458` with a 1px tactical outline.
- **Critical Narrative Branch:** Contained within a semi-transparent HUD card with an inked 1px border. Hovering initiates a subtle crimson or amber fill transition accompanied by a technical cursor crosshair.
- **Disabled Actions:** Muted dark surface (`#1A1C22`), cross-hatch diagonal slash pattern overlay, and dimmed Space Mono status labels indicating missing resources.

### Tactical HUD & Vital Chips
- **Resource Chips:** Compact rectangular indicators with 1px border framing an icon, monospaced value, and a 2px micro-progress bar representing biological decay (Hunger, Hydration, Exposure, Infection).
- **Survival Countdown Clock (The 72h Gauge):** A prominent countdown HUD component using Space Mono bold numbers flanked by a segmented mechanical progress rail. Color dynamically shifts from Tactical Amber to Hazard Crimson when below 12 hours.

### Inventory & Equipment Cards
- **Grid Item Slots:** Strict square or 4:3 cards with `#1A1C22` semi-transparent backgrounds and etched borders. Equipped or active items feature an amber corner dog-ear badge. Durability meters sit flush along the bottom edge as 2px solid lines.

### Narrative Dialogue & Log Lists
- **Text Logs:** Monospaced transcripts tagged with absolute time stamps (`[071:42:15]`) in Cyan (`#4A90E2`). Speaker identifiers are bracketed in uppercase Barlow Condensed.
- **Choice Lists:** Stacked vertical options separated by 1px graphic dividers. Focused options trigger an active left border tick in amber with an audio-visual click effect.

### Input & Radio Selectors
- **Tactical Checkboxes:** Square checkboxes with an inner micro-diamond or tactical crosshair fill rather than a rounded tick mark.
- **Radio Buttons:** Octagonal or chamfered square nodes with an inner dot indicator.
- **Code Inputs & Keypads:** Monospaced segmented input blocks, simulating rugged hardware membrane keys with immediate, crisp state transitions.
